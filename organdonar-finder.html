About Organ Donation

Organ donation is the process of donating healthy organs to people who need them for medical treatment. It can give another person a chance at life. Our website is a student project designed to spread awareness about organ donation and demonstrate a donor information search system.

❤️ Heart

Heart donation can help patients with serious heart conditions.

🫘 Kidney

Kidney transplantation can help people with kidney failure.

🫁 Lungs

Lung transplantation may help people with severe lung diseases.

🧬 Liver

Liver transplantation can help patients with serious liver disease.
